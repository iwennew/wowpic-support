常见问题

作品在哪里？

作品保存在 App 本地。建议定期导出文件备份，并检查备份文件能否读取。

删除后可以找回吗？

App 没有回收站。确认删除后无法在 App 中撤销；如有事先导出的备份，可尝试导入该副本。

为什么不能保存到相册？

请检查系统设置中的照片添加权限以及设备剩余空间。

需要账号或订阅吗？

首版无需账号，免费使用。

Frequently asked questions

Where is my work stored?

In the app's local storage. Export regular file backups and verify that they can be read.

Can I undo deletion?

There is no recently deleted folder. Confirmed deletion cannot be undone in the app. If you previously exported a backup, you can try importing that copy.

Why can't I save to Photos?

Check photo-add permissions in system settings and available device storage.

Do I need an account or subscription?

The first release is free and requires no account.
