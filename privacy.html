wowpic 如何处理你的数据

wowpic 的核心图片编辑在设备上完成。你选择导入的图片、创作工程、草稿和素材保存在 App 的本地存储中。首版无需注册账号，不提供应用内云备份。

wowpic 使用系统照片选择器导入你选择的图片。在你将作品保存到相册时，App 会请求添加照片的权限。你可以在系统设置中管理相关权限。

我们不通过 App 将你的图片和工程上传到开发者服务器，不使用广告追踪或行为分析服务。你主动导出、分享或将备份保存至第三方服务时，目标服务会按照其自身政策处理文件。系统设备备份及照片同步也取决于你的系统设置；“本地编辑”并不表示你选择的系统服务永远不会同步文件。

App 在设备上保存显示方式、排序和编辑偏好，以记住你的选择。确认删除作品后会永久删除相应的本地作品，App 不提供回收站。已另行导出、分享或备份的副本需要你到对应位置单独管理。请在删除 App 或更换设备前保留有效备份。

如果你主动联系支持，我们会收到你提供的邮件地址、问题说明及附件。这些资料仅用于处理问题及必要的后续沟通，不再需要时删除。你可以发送邮件到 iwennew@163.com 申请删除。请不要发送无关的私密图片或敏感资料。

若后续版本改变数据处理方式，我们会更新本政策。

English

How wowpic handles your data

Operator: Chenglong Wen (文成龙). Contact: iwennew@163.com.

Core image editing in wowpic takes place on your device. Images you import, projects, drafts, and materials are stored in the app's local storage. The first release does not require account registration or offer in-app cloud backup.

wowpic uses the system photo picker to import images you select. When you save artwork to Photos, the app requests permission to add photos. You can manage relevant permissions in system settings.

We do not upload your images or projects from the app to developer-operated servers, or use advertising tracking or behavioral analytics services. When you choose to export, share, or store a backup with another service, that service handles the files under its own policies. Device backups and photo synchronization also depend on your system settings. Local editing does not prevent synchronization by services you choose.

The app stores display, sorting, and editing preferences on your device. Confirming deletion permanently removes the corresponding local project; the app has no recently deleted folder. Copies you previously exported, shared, or backed up must be managed separately. Keep a valid backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

If you contact support, we receive the email address, description, and attachments you provide. We use this information only to handle your issue and for necessary follow-up, and delete it when it is no longer needed. You can request deletion by emailing iwennew@163.com. Please avoid sending unrelated private images or sensitive information.

We will update this policy if future versions change how data is handled.
